Franchise Opportunity Profile

Orangetheory Fitness

Orangetheory Fitness is a health and fitness studio franchise headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida. Every fact on this page is sourced from the OTF Franchisor, LLC Franchise Disclosure Document (issued March 31, 2026), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$59,950

Uniform, non-refundable. Area development: $150,000 (3 studios) / $237,500 (5 studios) for new franchisees. (FDD Item 5, p.9)

Estimated Initial Investment

$764,577 to $1,104,920

$182,658-$237,994 of that is payable to the franchisor or affiliates. Excludes real estate purchase costs. (FDD cover, p.1; Item 7, p.17)

Royalty

8% of Gross Sales

Payable weekly; franchisor debits the franchisee bank account. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Brand Fund

3% of Gross Sales

Payable monthly; may be increased up to 5%. Local advertising minimum: greater of 2% of prior-month gross sales or $2,500. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Technology Fee

$899/month

Management software setup $575; monthly technology fee currently $899, subject to 10% compounded annual increases. (FDD Item 5, p.10)

Territory

Non-exclusive

No exclusive territory, but the franchise agreement designates a protected Territory (Limited Access Location exceptions); minimum performance standards apply. (FDD Item 12, pp.43-44)

Performance Standards

$300K / $350K / $400K

Minimum gross sales Year 1 / Year 2 / Year 3+. Shortfall triggers a royalty true-up; two consecutive misses can mean territory reduction or termination. (FDD Item 12, p.44)

Term

10 years

From the effective date (parties may agree up to 11 years); successor franchise fee = 50% of then-current initial fee. (FDD Item 17, p.53; Item 6, p.12)

Facility

~3,100 sq ft typical

Range 1,750 to 4,800 sq ft; site selected and secured within 4 months of signing; franchisor acceptance required. (FDD Item 7, note 2, p.17; Item 12, p.43)

Training

4-day initial program

Held quarterly at HQ in Boca Raton with online portions; Studio Launch Training + presales training for first studio. (FDD Item 11)

Owner Model

Manager-run possible

Owner personal supervision recommended, not required; a full-time trained on-premises manager must run the Studio. (FDD Item 15, p.50)

HQ

Boca Raton, Florida

OTF Franchisor, LLC, 6000 Broken Sound Parkway NW, Suite 200, Boca Raton, FL 33487. (954) 530-6903. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

1,224 studios

1,209 franchised + 15 affiliate-owned as of 12/31/2025; net -74 in 2025, -35 in 2024. (FDD Item 20, p.60)

Risk Profile & Disclosure

60 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate-High. A large, established system with strong Item 19 gross-sales reporting, but the system has been contracting since 2023: total studios fell from 1,333 at the end of 2023 to 1,224 at the end of 2025, 95 studios permanently closed during the Item 19 period, and 362 franchisees appear on the left-the-system list. Capital intensity is high (investment ceiling $1,104,920), ongoing fees run 13–15% of gross sales, territories are non-exclusive with broad franchisor reserved rights (including acquiring and converting other businesses inside your Territory), and mandatory minimum performance standards carry a royalty true-up with termination risk after two consecutive misses.
